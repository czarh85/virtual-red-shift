2026-10-03 Beta build 2

Beta build 2 answers the reports of the first round. It goes to the same invited testers; there is still no download link.

BETA WARP starts a game anywhere in the story, from Space Station 1 to the Final Level.

Fixed

  • Teleporters: START to teleport; the last one of Space Station 1 takes you to the ship.
  • Jukebox: each console names its track; START plays it.
  • Spikes: they kill like in the original; you can walk along a spiked wall again.
  • Moving platforms: they never carry you into a wall.
  • Space Station 1: the bus near the end of the level is now a monster.
  • Space Station 2: the way in on foot is open again.
  • Comms Relay: the radar and Violet's call are back.
  • Background: it stays in place from room to room.
  • Saving: automatic at the end of each level and at each rescue.
  • Pause: QUIT TO TITLE asks first.
  • Time trials: they never touch your saved game.
  • Right D-pad: the hidden shortcut is gone.

New: BETA WARP

The levels and rescues, in story order. Pick one, answer YES: a new game starts there.

  • Open it: on the title screen, UP then A (it is the last line); in game, START then L.
  • Choose: UP and DOWN, then A; B goes back.
  • Confirm: NO comes first; DOWN, then A on YES.
  • EXTRAS: visit without saving, replay a scene, invincibility and game speed.

Lost your save?

Open BETA WARP, choose where you were, answer YES.

Reporting a bug

Any report helps. If you can, add:

  • the room code at the top right of the screen, for example S044;
  • what you did and what you saw;
  • the build label on the title screen;
  • a photo of the screen.

One message per bug.

2026-09-25 Beta build 1 ready for the private beta

The first beta build of VIRTUAL RED SHIFT is complete. It will be distributed to a limited group of invited testers. Whether a wider beta follows depends on this first session, and on the build staying private.

There is no download link. The beta build is sent directly to each invited tester.

Improvements

  • Cutscenes: 158 story scenes converted, 47 playing in this build.
  • Enemies: 34 room-specific designs across 43 rooms.
  • Map: drawn from the actual rooms, with fog of war, stereo depth and memory of explored rooms.
  • Menu: START opens the map; L and R switch between MAP, CREW, STATS and SAVE.
  • Music: 16 original chiptune tracks; none of Magnus Pålsson's soundtrack is used.
