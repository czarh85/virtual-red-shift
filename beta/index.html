2026-09-25 Beta build 1 ready for the private beta

The first beta build of VIRTUAL RED SHIFT is complete. It will be distributed to a limited group of invited testers. Whether a wider beta follows depends on this first session, and on the build staying private.

There is no download link. The beta build is sent directly to each invited tester.

  • Cutscenes: all 158 story scenes, 774 lines of dialogue.
  • Enemies: 34 room-specific designs across 43 rooms.
  • Map: drawn from the actual rooms, with fog of war, stereo depth and memory of explored rooms.
  • Menu: START opens the map; L and R switch between MAP, CREW, STATS and SAVE.
  • Music: 16 original, royalty-free chiptune tracks.

A private beta for a Virtual Boy game

VIRTUAL RED SHIFT is a homebrew game for the Nintendo Virtual Boy, played in red and in stereo depth. Before its public release, a small group of testers will play it on real hardware or in an emulator and report what breaks.

About this port

VIRTUAL RED SHIFT is an unofficial, modified port based on the VVVVVV source code, © 2010-2020 Terry Cavanagh. It is not the original game. It is non-commercial and will never be sold: if you paid for it, you were scammed. VVVVVV is a commercial game; support its author at thelettervsixtim.es.
