A closed beta for a Virtual Boy game

VIRTUAL RED SHIFT is an unofficial Virtual Boy port of Terry Cavanagh's VVVVVV, played in red and in stereo depth. A small group of testers plays it on real hardware or in an emulator and reports what breaks. Anything here is removed at the author's request.

To join the closed beta, send a private message on Discord to @henzelince.

About this port

VIRTUAL RED SHIFT is an unofficial, modified port based on the VVVVVV source code, © 2010-2020 Terry Cavanagh. It is not the original game. It is non-commercial and will never be sold: if you paid for it, you were scammed. VVVVVV is a commercial game; support its author at thelettervsixtim.es. Music by Magnus Pålsson, not included in this port. 2.0 C++ port by Simon Roth; 2.2 SDL2 port by Ethan Lee. Room names by Bennett Foddy. How it's made.

No cookies. No analytics. No third-party scripts.

Other Virtual Boy games: Red Panic: an original game for the Virtual Boy, with a free demo. Virtual Breakout: a stereoscopic brick-breaker, free to download.
